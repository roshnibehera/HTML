About the Event

TechNova 2026 is the annual technical and cultural festival organized by Horizon Institute of Technology. The event brings together students from different colleges to participate in exciting competitions, workshops, and cultural performances. It is a platform to showcase talent, creativity, teamwork, and innovation.
