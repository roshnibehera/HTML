How to make pizza

Ingredients:

For the dough:

  • 2 cups(250g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup (180 ml) warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil

For the toppings:

  1. ½ cup pizza sauce
  2. 1 ½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  3. Toppings of your choice (pepperoni, mushrooms, onions, bell peppers, olives, tomatoes, etc.)
  4. 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  5. ½ teaspoon chili flakes (optional)
